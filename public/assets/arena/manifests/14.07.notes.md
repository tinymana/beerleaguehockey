# Batch 14.07 — Arena lighting
Use the atlas or individual RGBA sprites, nearest-neighbor integer scaling. Mount fixtures by anchor [16,2] on structural beams outside the playable ice. Swap matching on/off states without changing position. Render optional light_glow.png separately beneath skaters and above ice, with adjustable opacity; do not bake it into ice tiles. Keep lighting decorative, with no physics or scoring changes. Test fixture scale, placement and readability in Arena Asset Lab. Preview is not a runtime asset.
