# Beer League Hockey — 10.01 West straight boards
Load the atlas as 8×8 tiles, no spacing or margin. Assemble row-major into a 24×32 module and repeat vertically along the west boundary. Ice is on the right. Align local x=19 with the existing west ice boundary; do not change collision geometry. Use nearest-neighbor filtering and integer coordinates. Body and kickplate can alternatively be composited at offsets (0,0) and (16,0); do not render both the complete module and its parts. Glass, corners, gates and crowds are separate future batches. Preview is not a runtime asset. Dimensions and palette are provisional; validate against existing rink in-game.
